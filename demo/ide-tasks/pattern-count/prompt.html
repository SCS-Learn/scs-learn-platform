Bacteria begin replicating their genome at the replication origin (ori), where the protein DnaA binds to short, repeated DnaA boxes. A k-mer that shows up surprisingly often in a short window is a hint that we have found one.

Write pattern_count(text, pattern), which returns the number of times pattern appears in text. Overlapping occurrences count: GCG appears 2 times in GCGCG.

Sample input: pattern_count("GCGCG", "GCG")
Sample output: 2
