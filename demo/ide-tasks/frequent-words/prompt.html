A k-mer is a most frequent k-mer in text if no other k-mer appears more often. In real ori regions, the DnaA boxes are often among the most frequent 9-mers.

Write frequent_words(text, k), which returns a list of all most frequent k-mers in text, each listed once, in any order.

Sample input: frequent_words("ACGTTGCATGTCGCATGATGCATGAGAGCT", 4)
Sample output: ["CATG", "GCAT"]
